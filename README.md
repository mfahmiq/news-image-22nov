One image summing up the world's news of Sunday 22 November 2026: the cargo ship Sparta II ablaze in the Gulf of Finland after a suspected drone strike, two oil tankers struck near the Strait of Hormuz, COP31 climate talks in Antalya going into a second overtime day with no deal, Iran claiming a missile hit a US F-16 while US Central Command denies it, cost-of-living protests clashing with police in Strasbourg, and a magnitude 6.7 earthquake in Peru's Junin region.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```

The program draws the image with Pillow (standard library + PIL only, no network) and writes `entry.png` — a 1024×1024 picture with a navy date header, a 2×3 grid of story panels, each with a headline, a short factual summary and a small illustration drawn from plain shapes.
